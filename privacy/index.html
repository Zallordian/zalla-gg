Privacy Policy

How Zalla handles data on your device.

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Developer: Kurt Paris (doing business as Zalla)

Contact: ZallaBrowser@pm.me

Zalla is a privacy-conscious web browser for iPhone. This policy explains what happens when you use the Zalla app. It is written for App Store users and reviewers.

Short version

Zalla does not create a Zalla account. Zalla does not run its own analytics or advertising SDK. Browsing data you keep in Zalla (tabs, bookmarks, history, downloads, home shortcuts, and settings) stays on your device unless you export it yourself. Websites you visit may still collect data under their own policies. That is normal for any browser.

Who we are

Zalla is published by Kurt Paris as an Individual Apple Developer. For privacy questions, email ZallaBrowser@pm.me.

Information Zalla stores on your device

Depending on how you use the app, Zalla may store on your iPhone:

  • Browsing activity: page titles and URLs for history (non-private tabs), open tabs, and back/forward lists
  • Library: bookmarks you save or import
  • Downloads: files you download through Zalla, plus a local downloads list
  • Home / new tab: shortcuts, recent chips preferences, and related personalization settings
  • Preferences: appearance, accent theme, search engine, toolbar style, app icon preference, onboarding completion, and similar settings
  • Privacy Report: running totals of what Zalla did for you (links cleaned, secure connections upgraded, cookie banners closed) and which sites they happened on. It never records private tabs and never leaves your device
  • Tab previews: short-lived in-memory snapshots used for the tab switcher (not uploaded by Zalla)

Private tabs use a non-persistent website data store so they do not keep the same lasting website data as normal tabs.

Zalla does not sync this library to Zalla servers. There is no Zalla cloud account.

Widgets. Zalla’s widgets read a small note that the app keeps in an App Group, a storage area that only Zalla and its widgets on your iPhone can use. The note holds your accent color, your favorites (home shortcuts and bookmarks), and your Privacy Report totals. It never holds your history, your open tabs, or anything from a private tab. Widgets do not use the network, and nothing in the note is sent to Zalla. Turn off Share with widgets in Settings, Tools, Widgets and Zalla erases the note.

Information you may share with Apple or websites

  • Websites: When you load a page, the site (and parties it uses) can receive your IP address, device details, cookies, and whatever you enter on that site. Zalla cannot rewrite every site’s privacy practices. Use Private tabs, clear browsing data, and choose search engines carefully if you want less tracking.
  • Search engines: Searches you run through Zalla’s address bar are sent to the search engine you pick in Settings. That engine’s privacy policy applies.
  • Apple services: System features you opt into, such as saving an exported image to Photos, AutoFill / Apple Passwords, Share Sheet destinations, or iCloud Keychain, are handled by Apple under Apple’s policies. Zalla asks for Photos access only when you choose Save to Photos for Export As.
  • Tips and Zalla Unlock: Tips and Zalla Unlock are in-app purchases handled by Apple through the App Store. Zalla gets no payment details, and Zalla has no server that receives them. Zalla asks Apple whether you own Zalla Unlock, and Restore Purchases (Settings, Premium, Zalla Unlock) asks Apple again, so a purchase follows your Apple Account to a new iPhone. Tips never unlock anything. A note that you have tipped stays on your device.
  • Apple Maps (optional city lookup): If you type a city or town in Settings, Privacy, Location, Zalla sends that name once to Apple Maps place search to find the center of the city. Nothing else is sent, and your device’s location is not used. Apple handles that request under Apple’s policies. The center point is saved on your device only.
  • Cloudflare (Network Speed test): Only when you tap Start in Settings, Tools, Network Speed, Zalla downloads and uploads test data with Cloudflare’s public speed test servers. It does not run unless you tap Start. Cloudflare can see your IP address, as any site you connect to can, and its privacy policy applies. Your results stay on your device.
  • Cloudflare or Quad9 (optional encrypted DNS): Off by default. If you turn on Encrypted DNS in Settings, Privacy, Privacy Shield, Zalla looks up website addresses for its own requests, such as image export and the speed test, through the provider you choose, Cloudflare or Quad9, over an encrypted connection. That provider can see which names are looked up from your IP address, and its privacy policy applies. Web pages in Zalla keep using your iPhone’s normal address lookups, because iOS does not let an app change that.
  • App Store / TestFlight: Apple collects standard App Store and TestFlight diagnostics and purchase information under Apple’s terms.

What we do not collect

As of this policy, Zalla itself does not:

  • Require or offer a Zalla login
  • Operate a Zalla analytics, crash, or advertising backend that receives your browsing history
  • Sell personal information
  • Build advertising profiles about you
  • Collect, store, or receive your device’s location. Location is used on your device and shared only with a website you allow.

If that changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change ships.

Permissions

  • Photos (add only): Used only if you choose Save to Photos when exporting an image.
  • Location (optional, only when a site asks): Some websites ask where you are, for things like weather and nearby businesses. By default Zalla asks you first, for each site. In Settings, Privacy, Website location, you can choose Never to block every request. If you allow a site, your iPhone’s location is shared with that site only, through Apple’s web engine. It does not go to Zalla, Zalla has no server that could receive it, and Zalla does not store a copy. Zalla remembers only your Allow or Don’t Allow answer for each site, on your device, so it can skip asking next time. You can see and remove those answers in Settings, Privacy, Location. Private tabs ask every time and remember nothing. You can also turn location off for Zalla at any time in iOS Settings. The separate “city” setting in Zalla is text you type yourself and never uses your device’s location.
  • Face ID (optional, Zalla Unlock): If you turn on Face ID for private tabs in Settings, Premium, Zalla asks iOS to check that it is you, using Face ID with your device passcode as the backup. iOS does the check and tells Zalla only yes or no. Zalla never gets your face data or any other biometric data, and none of it leaves your device.
  • Camera and microphone (only when a site asks): When a site asks to use your camera or microphone, Zalla shows a prompt naming the site, and iOS may ask as well. If you allow it, the camera or microphone goes to that site only, through Apple’s web engine (WebKit). It does not go to Zalla. Zalla remembers your answer for that site only until you close the app, and does not save it to disk. Private tabs keep their own answers. You can turn camera and microphone off for Zalla at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Network: Used to load websites and downloads you request.
  • Local files / shares: Used when you import or export bookmarks HTML, share a page or file, or save downloads.

Burn It All

Burn It All is a button that erases your browsing data on this device and starts over on one fresh tab. It asks you to confirm first, and the app does not quit.

What it erases:

  • Every open tab, private tabs included, and the saved list of tabs, so nothing is restored next launch
  • History
  • Cookies, website data, and caches
  • Saved per-site answers for location, camera, and microphone, plus saved page zoom and desktop site choices
  • Which sites your Privacy Report counts belong to

What stays:

  • Bookmarks
  • Downloads
  • Settings and preferences
  • Your Privacy Report totals

If Zalla is closed partway through, it finishes the job the next time it opens.

Children

Zalla is a general-audience browser. It is not directed at children under 13. Do not use Zalla to collect personal information from children.

Your choices

In Settings (and related screens) you can:

  • Clear browsing data (Settings, Privacy, Clear browsing data): closes all tabs and removes history, cookies, website caches, and saved page zoom levels. Bookmarks and downloads are kept
  • Use Burn It All, described above
  • Reset the App (Settings, Privacy, Reset the App): restores settings to their defaults, clears downloads and your Privacy Report totals, and keeps bookmarks
  • Delete downloads from the Downloads list (Settings, Tools, Downloads)
  • Remove bookmarks and change home shortcuts
  • Uninstall Zalla, which removes on-device app data subject to iOS behavior

Third-party links and content

Zalla displays third-party websites. Those sites are not controlled by Zalla. Their content, cookies, and policies are their responsibility.

International users

Zalla is offered through the App Store. Data you keep in the app remains on your device. Contact us if you have a privacy request we can reasonably fulfill for an Individual developer app with no Zalla account system.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s behavior changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy for that version.
