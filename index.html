Privacy

Private by default. Every protection has a switch.

Protections start on, and you can turn any of them off when a site needs it. Zalla has no account and no analytics, so there is nothing for us to collect.

  • Tracker and ad blockingBuilt on EasyList based rules, with per-site allow lists for the sites you want to support.
  • Burn It AllOne button wipes your browsing data, plays a short fire animation, and drops you on a fresh tab.
  • Privacy ShieldEncrypted DNS for Zalla's own requests, tracking parameter stripping, fingerprinting protection, referrer trimming, and an optional proxy you supply.
  • Local-firstHistory, bookmarks and settings stay on your iPhone. No sign-in, no sync server, nothing sent to us.
  • Face ID for private tabsLock your private tabs behind Face ID. Included with Zalla Unlock.

Zalla is not a VPN. Privacy Shield does not hide your IP address or route your traffic through our servers. We do not run any servers for that. If you already use a proxy, you can point Zalla at it.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Is Zalla a VPN?

No. Privacy Shield is a set of in-browser protections like encrypted DNS and tracking parameter stripping. It does not hide your IP address or tunnel your traffic. If you already use a proxy, you can enter it in settings and Zalla will use it for pages you load.

Does Zalla collect my data?

No. There is no account, no analytics and no third party SDKs. Your history, bookmarks and settings stay on your iPhone. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I turn the blocking off?

Yes. Every protection has its own toggle, and you can allow individual sites. Privacy is the default, not a requirement.

What does Burn It All delete?

Your tabs, history, cookies and site data. Bookmarks, downloads and settings stay. It asks first, plays the fire (or a quick fade, if you turn the fire off), and opens a fresh tab.

Does it have a password manager?

Zalla does not store passwords itself. It works with iOS AutoFill, so Apple Passwords and other AutoFill providers fill in logins as they do elsewhere.

What do I get with Zalla Unlock?

The premium themes and the extra features listed above, for a one-time $1.99. It works with Family Sharing, and Restore Purchases is in the Unlock screen.

Which devices does it run on?
