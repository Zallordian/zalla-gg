Descriptions

Short

Private by default. Beautiful by design. Free tracker blocking, no account, and your history stays on your iPhone. A browser that minds its own business.

Facts

  • Name: Zalla
  • Platform: iPhone, iOS 17 or later
  • Price: free, with an optional one-time $1.99 Zalla Unlock
  • Developer: Kurt Paris
  • Website: zalla.gg
Long description

Zalla is a private, local-first web browser for iPhone. Built around you.

Private by default. Beautiful by design.

There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics. Your history, bookmarks and settings live on your iPhone, which is conveniently where you are.

Privacy, without the homework

  • Blocks known trackers and common ad networks, free and on by default
  • HTTPS-Only mode, on by default, with a clear warning before an insecure page
  • Private tabs with their own throwaway website storage, kept out of your history
  • Cookie banner closer that only ever picks reject or necessary, never accept
  • Privacy Report that counts what Zalla actually did: links cleaned, HTTPS upgrades and banners closed. No made-up "trackers blocked" number
  • Website location is Ask by default, or Never. A site you allow gets your location through Apple's web engine. Zalla itself never receives it

Privacy Shield (not a VPN)

  • Cleans tracking tags like utm_source and fbclid out of links
  • Trims the referrer, so the next site sees where you came from, not the exact page
  • Optional fingerprinting protection
  • Optional encrypted DNS for Zalla's own requests. iOS does not let an app change this for web pages, and we would rather say so than pretend
  • Use your own HTTP or SOCKS5 proxy, if you have one

Privacy Shield does not hide your IP address. Sites and your search engine still see the requests you make.

Burn It All

One button closes every tab, erases history, cookies and site data, then opens a fresh tab so nothing lingers. It asks first. Bookmarks and downloads stay put. The fire effect is optional.

Search your way

  • Pick from several popular search engines, or add your own
  • Press and hold the engine label to switch
  • Optional city for local searches, typed by you and stored on your iPhone. No GPS

Make it yours

  • Address bar on top or bottom, with Classic, Compact or Quick Action toolbars
  • Themes, a custom accent color and matching app icons
  • New tab page with your shortcuts, backgrounds and short sayings
  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: Search, Favorites, Burn It All and Privacy Report
  • Quick actions on the app icon

Everyday tools

  • Reader mode, find on page, page zoom and request desktop site
  • Downloads, plus Export As for images (PNG, JPEG and HEIC where your iPhone supports them)
  • Tab restore, swipe to close tabs, pull to refresh, edge swipe back and forward
  • Network speed test, run only when you tap it
  • Works with Apple Passwords through AutoFill. Zalla has no password vault of its own, on purpose

Zalla Unlock (one time, no subscription)

The browser is free. One optional purchase adds:

  • Full ad blocking, hidden ad spaces, and cookie banner and annoyance lists
  • Hide Element on any site, plus custom block and allow rules
  • Face ID for private tabs
  • Tab groups, listen to page and per-site CSS
  • Scheduled auto-clear
  • Background packs and eight animated theme packs with matching icons

There is also a tip jar. Tips are a thank you and unlock nothing, apart from our gratitude.
